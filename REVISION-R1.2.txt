R1.2 inward-stacked female sockets — 2026-10-14

J2, J3 and J4 use 2.54mm vertical female sockets. J3 is in the upper-right area;
J2 is lower-right and oriented with pin 1 TX at the top and pin 5 VCC at the bottom.
J2 front-view top-to-bottom order: TX, RX, OUT, GND, VCC.
LD2410C is intended to plug in with its radar antenna face upward and its body
extending inward over the mainboard. This supersedes the unpublished R1.1 outward
layout. J2 pin1 centre: (141.4,127) mm; J3 pin1: (141.4,108) mm in KiCad coordinates.
Mainboard outline remains 45 x 50mm; the module envelope is intended to remain
within it. The 16 x 22mm module outline in assembly-guide.svg is an estimate using
a centred pin row. Exact pin-to-edge offsets, component underside clearance,
socket height, male pin insertion and final enclosure size require sample checks.
The illustration is not a verified mechanical model and does not certify RF
coexistence. Wi-Fi/CSI/radar tests and first hardware power-up remain pending.
J2: Wurth 61300511821; J3/J4: 61300411821; drill 1.02mm. J1 remains USB4105-GF-A.
No electrical pin definitions or firmware rules have changed. C5 was repositioned
to make room for J2. Prior ideal reset-RC simulation and six software rule tests
are reused; current ERC/DRC/netlist checks are regenerated.
